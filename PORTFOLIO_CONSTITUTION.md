# Portfolio Constitution

The narrative soul of the portfolio — how the already-approved section arc reads as a book, what the site believes, and the manifesto behind it. This document doesn't govern _how the studio works_ (that's `EDITORIAL_OS.md`, still the highest authority) — it governs _what the portfolio is trying to be felt as_. Where the two touch (accessibility, restraint, consistency), they agree; `EDITORIAL_OS.md` wins on process, this document wins on narrative intent.

Governing test for every future decision on this portfolio: **does this make the portfolio feel more like Stéphania?** If not, reject it.

**Status: frozen as of 2026-07-08**, cross-referenced from `EDITORIAL_OS.md`. Frozen means the architecture and structural decisions below are settled and not open for casual revision — reopening requires the same deliberateness that created them. It does not mean every item in this document is finished: the Manifesto is explicitly a first draft, not final or authentic voice until reviewed, and the items under **Open / Future-proof** below remain genuinely open. ## Portfolio Story

A visitor doesn't read a résumé; they move through an argument: _here is what I've made_ → _here is what I believe and how I work_ → _here is who I am_ → _here is how to reach me_. Each chapter earns the next — Chapter I's proof is what makes Chapter II's beliefs credible rather than aspirational.

## Core Philosophy

- People before patterns.
- Accessibility is respect.
- Consistency builds confidence.
- Beauty follows clarity.
- Products should earn trust.
- I design products people can rely on.

**How these become visible**: not as stated text — as behavior the site already performs. This portfolio already passes WCAG A/AA on every change (an automated gate, not a claim), already has real focus states, already has real semantic structure. A belief that has to announce itself with a callout is weaker than one nobody had to point at. The one exception is accessibility specifically: its visibility is to the people who need it — a keyboard user sees the real focus ring, a screen reader user hears the real structure — not a demonstrative gesture for sighted visitors who don't need proof of it.

## Manifesto — _first draft, awaiting her real voice_

> I design products people can rely on.
>
> Not products that impress once and fail under real use. Products that hold — for the person who needs the accessible path, not just the easy one; for the team that inherits the system six months after I've moved on; for the business that needs the decision defended, not just delivered.
>
> People come before patterns. A pattern that serves the system at the person's expense is a pattern I won't ship. Accessibility isn't a feature I add — it's the respect a product owes everyone who uses it, present in the first decision, not the last review. Consistency isn't a style choice — it's how a product earns the right to be trusted the second time, and the hundredth.
>
> Beauty follows clarity. It doesn't lead it, and it never excuses its absence.
>
> This is not a portfolio of things I made. It's evidence of what I believe a product owes the people who depend on it.

This is a synthesis of the six core-philosophy statements above, not yet her actual words. It should still feel true in ten years — that's the bar, not "sounds inspirational today."

## What I Believe

Not a new page — this site has no router and no multi-page capability, and a new page would contradict the "no new page" principle this sprint was scoped under. "What I Believe" is Chapter II under its existing name: Design Principles + Selected Thoughts, read together.

## Chapter Structure, Transitions, Emotional Journey

Each chapter keeps the visual/reading rhythm already decided in prior sprints (Hero's value-first hierarchy, Work's editorial proof-first structure, the Art Direction and Editorial Assets documents) — this document adds narrative naming, not new visual rules. Chapter breaks stay the existing chapter-ritual kicker + hairline (`EDITORIAL_OS.md` §7.2), plus Editorial Quote's deliberate wordless pause between Chapter I and Chapter II — no new transition device.

Emotional arc: Prologue is earned calm. Chapter I is proof-earned confidence. Chapter II is quiet conviction, stated plainly, without persuasion effort. Chapter III is the one permitted warmth shift (About is the one section where a portrait belongs). Epilogue is openness without eagerness.

## "What I took with me" (per case study, closing section)

Structure only — the actual reflections aren't written yet, since they require real personal reflection this document can't fabricate. Tone: one sentence, same register as the rest of the site — a specific, concrete change in how she designs _now_, not a feeling about the project _then_. Not "lessons learned." Not sentimental. As disciplined as everything else this portfolio says about itself.

## Recognition Strategy

Unchanged from the Art Direction and Editorial Assets documents — the burgundy's rarity, the editorial ritual, the restraint. This document adds narrative structure, not a new visual device to recognize the site by.
