06 — Approach

05 stages
I

Discover

Listening — to users, to data, to the constraints nobody wrote down.

II

Frame

Complexity is rarely the problem — ambiguity is. Scattered inputs into one point of view.

III

Design

Components, patterns and language that stay coherent as the product grows.

IV

Validate

Design is a hypothesis until it meets real people. Accessibility is part of "working".

V

Scale

Finished when a team can carry it forward without me — docs, tokens, shared language.
